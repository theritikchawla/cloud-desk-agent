# Troubleshooting Guide

## Supported browsers
CloudDesk works on the latest two versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. Internet Explorer is not supported.

## Pages or boards load slowly
First refresh the page. Then clear your browser cache, turn off browser extensions, or try a private window. Check the status page at status.clouddesk.example to see if there is a known problem.

## Not receiving notifications
Check Settings > Notifications to make sure the notification type is switched on. Check your spam folder for emails. For desktop notifications, make sure your browser allows notifications from CloudDesk. Email digests are sent at 8:00 AM in the workspace time zone.

## File upload fails
The most common reasons are the file being too large or the workspace storage being full. The maximum file size is 25 MB on Free, 250 MB on Pro and 1 GB on Business. Try a smaller file or free up storage by deleting old attachments.

## Integrations disconnected
Go to Settings > Integrations and reconnect the app. A Slack connection stops working if a Slack admin removes the CloudDesk app from the Slack workspace.

## Mobile app problems
Update to the latest version of the app, sign out and sign in again, and reinstall the app if the problem continues.

## Data not syncing between devices
Check that each device has an internet connection. On mobile, pull down on the board to refresh.

## Contacting support
Email support@clouddesk.example. Support is by email only and is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 UTC. We aim to reply within 1 business day for Free, within 4 business hours for Pro and Business, and Enterprise customers have a dedicated account manager.
