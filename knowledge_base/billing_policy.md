# Subscription Plans, Billing and Refunds

## Plans and prices
CloudDesk has four plans. All prices are in US dollars and exclude taxes.
- Free: $0.
- Pro: $12 per user per month when billed monthly, or $10 per user per month when billed annually.
- Business: $24 per user per month when billed monthly, or $20 per user per month when billed annually.
- Enterprise: custom pricing. Contact sales@clouddesk.example.

## Free trial
Every new workspace gets a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan. No payment card is required. When the trial ends, the workspace moves to the Free plan automatically unless the owner upgrades.

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. Annual Business plans can also pay by invoice with bank transfer. CloudDesk support will never ask you for your full card number, your CVV code or your password.

## Billing cycle and invoices
You are charged at the start of each billing cycle, on the same day you subscribed. Invoices are emailed to the workspace owner and are available in Settings > Billing > Invoices. To add a VAT ID, go to Settings > Billing.

## Adding and removing seats
When you add seats in the middle of a billing cycle, you are charged a prorated amount for the remaining days. When you remove seats, the change takes effect at the next renewal and no credit is given for the current period.

## Upgrading and downgrading
Upgrades take effect immediately and the price difference is prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Cancelling a subscription
You can cancel at any time in Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for. After that the workspace moves to the Free plan. If the workspace is over the Free plan limits, the extra projects become read-only. Data is not deleted for 90 days.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, we retry up to 3 times over 7 days and email the workspace owner. If the payment still fails, the subscription is paused and the workspace becomes read-only until the payment method is updated.

## Refund policy
- Monthly plans: you can request a refund within 7 days of your first purchase. Monthly renewals are not refundable.
- Annual plans: you can request a full refund within 30 days of the purchase or renewal date.
- After these periods there are no refunds and no prorated refunds for unused time.
- Duplicate or incorrect charges: we refund these in full if you report them within 60 days of the charge.
- Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.
- Only the workspace owner or a billing admin can request a refund, in Settings > Billing > Request refund, or by emailing support@clouddesk.example.
- Enterprise contracts follow the terms of the signed contract.
