# CloudDesk Product Features

## Overview
CloudDesk is a project management platform for teams. You organise work in workspaces, projects, boards and tasks. CloudDesk is available on the web and as iOS and Android mobile apps. There is no desktop app.

## Boards and tasks
Every project has a Kanban board and a list view. Tasks support subtasks, assignees, due dates, labels, comments, @mentions and file attachments. You can drag tasks between columns to update their status.

## Timeline view
The Timeline view is a Gantt-style chart that shows tasks on a calendar with dependencies. Timeline is available on the Pro, Business and Enterprise plans. It is not available on the Free plan. On the mobile apps the Timeline is read-only.

## Time tracking
Time tracking is available on Pro, Business and Enterprise. You can start and stop a timer on any task, add time manually, and export weekly timesheets as CSV files.

## Automations
Automations let you create rules such as "when a task moves to Done, post a message in Slack". Automations are available on Business and Enterprise only. Business includes 1,000 automation runs per month. Enterprise has no run limit.

## Integrations
Slack, GitHub, Google Calendar and Google Drive integrations are available on all plans. The Microsoft Teams integration is available on Pro and above. You manage integrations in Settings > Integrations.

## API access
The CloudDesk REST API is available on Pro and above. The limit is 100 requests per minute per workspace. Create API tokens in Settings > Developer. Keep your tokens secret and never share them in tickets or chats.

## Guests and security features
Two-factor authentication is available on all plans. Guest access lets you invite external collaborators to specific projects. Guests are free and unlimited on Pro and above, and are not available on the Free plan. Single sign-on (SSO) with SAML is available on Enterprise only.

## Limits by plan
- Free: up to 5 members, 3 active projects, 2 GB workspace storage, 25 MB maximum file size.
- Pro: unlimited members and projects, 100 GB workspace storage, 250 MB maximum file size.
- Business: unlimited members and projects, 1 TB workspace storage, 1 GB maximum file size.
- Enterprise: custom limits agreed in the contract.
