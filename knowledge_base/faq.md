# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is there a free plan?
Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes up to 5 members, 3 active projects and 2 GB of storage.

## Can I try paid features before paying?
Yes. Every new workspace gets a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan with no payment card required.

## Can I export my data?
Yes. Owners and Admins can export workspace data as CSV or JSON in Settings > Workspace > Export.

## Is my data encrypted?
Workspace data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

## Does CloudDesk have a desktop app?
No. CloudDesk is available on the web and as iOS and Android apps.

## Which languages does CloudDesk support?
The CloudDesk interface is currently available in English only.

## Do you offer phone support?
No. Support is by email only at support@clouddesk.example.

## How do I contact sales?
Email sales@clouddesk.example for Enterprise plans and custom pricing.

## How many people can I invite on the Free plan?
The Free plan allows up to 5 members. Upgrade to Pro or Business for unlimited members.
