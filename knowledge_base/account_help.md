# Account Creation and Login Help

## Creating an account
Go to the CloudDesk sign-up page and register with your email address and a password, or sign in with Google. Passwords must be at least 12 characters long. You must verify your email address within 24 hours using the link we send you. The person who creates a workspace becomes its Owner.

## Logging in
Log in with your email and password, or with Google if you signed up that way. If you have two-factor authentication turned on, you will also be asked for a code from your authenticator app.

## Password reset
Click "Forgot password" on the login page and enter your account email. We send a reset link that is valid for 60 minutes and can be used only once. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder and wait 5 minutes before requesting another one. If you signed in with Google, reset your password through your Google account instead. CloudDesk support will never ask for your password.

## Account lockout
After 5 failed login attempts your account is locked for 15 minutes. After 15 minutes you can try again, or you can use the password reset link at any time.

## Two-factor authentication
Turn on two-factor authentication in Settings > Security using an authenticator app. When you set it up, you receive 10 one-time backup codes. If you lose your phone, log in with a backup code. If you have no backup codes, contact support. Identity verification can take up to 2 business days.

## Changing your email address
Go to Settings > Profile and enter your new email address. We send a confirmation link to the new address. Your email does not change until you click that link.

## Inviting teammates and roles
Owners and Admins invite people in Settings > Members. Invitations expire after 7 days. Roles are Owner, Admin, Member and Guest.

## Deleting your account
Go to Settings > Profile > Delete account. Deletion becomes permanent after a 14-day grace period. You can cancel the deletion by logging in during those 14 days.
